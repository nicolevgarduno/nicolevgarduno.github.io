As I've progressed in research, keeping up with the latest papers, updates, and news has become difficult.

Research Agent is a standing filter to make my life easier. You can describe the topics you care about and it goes out, gathers new research and news against those interests, and sends back a single daily email digest with a few sentences. The point is that it runs on its own schedule and arrives to you inbox rather than being one more feed to remember to check.
