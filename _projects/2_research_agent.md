Keeping up with a field moves from "nice to have" to "impossible" pretty quickly — new preprints land faster than anyone can triage them, and the signal you actually care about is a thin slice of the volume.

Research Agent is a standing filter for that problem. You describe the topics you care about; it goes out, gathers new research and news against those interests, and sends back a single daily email digest instead of a firehose. The point is that it runs on its own schedule and arrives where you already are, rather than being one more feed to remember to check.
